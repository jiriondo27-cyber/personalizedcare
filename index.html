Navigating Your Care Journey

1. Direct Initial Contact

Reach out via phone or online inquiry to discuss your immediate needs, concerns, or questions with a Registered Nurse.

2. In-Home Consultation

Stefanie conducts a thorough in-person assessment in your home to evaluate safety, medical needs, and specific care preferences.

Before Your Appointment

Contact your primary care provider and ask them to fax a referral to 860-926-2911 at least 48–72 hours before your visit. The referral should include your demographic information, the most recent office note, and your current medication list.

Gather all prescription bottles, over-the-counter medications, vitamins, and supplements so they are ready for review at the time of your visit.

3. Provider Coordination & Plan

We work in close coordination with your primary physician or medical team to align our nursing care plan directly with your existing prescriptions and treatment goals.

4. Ongoing Care & Oversight

Receive scheduled, high-quality nursing visits, routine symptom monitoring, and regular updates shared directly with family and physicians.

Our Background & History

Established in 2026

Personalized Home Care, LLC may be a young company, but it is built on a strong foundation of experience, dedication, and compassion. Our owner brings 8 years of hands‑on expertise in the home‑care industry, providing high‑quality, client‑centered nursing services throughout Connecticut.

This company was created to offer a more personal, reliable, and professional approach to in‑home care—one where every client is treated with dignity, respect, and individualized attention. Although our business is new, our commitment to excellence is backed by nearly a decade of real‑world clinical experience and a genuine passion for helping individuals remain safe, supported, and independent at home.
